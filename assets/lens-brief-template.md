## Checklist｜交付前检查

- [ ] The decision frame names a real decision, not a topic
- [ ] Exactly one primary lens; at most one secondary
- [ ] Access labels and descriptors are explicitly marked as *not* the segmentation
- [ ] At least one tempting cut is rejected, with a reason
- [ ] 2–4 contrast cards, every one tagged `[H]`, every one with a falsifier
- [ ] Matrix cells that would get the same marketing response have been merged
- [ ] No fictional names, quotes, percentages or segment sizes anywhere
- [ ] Facts, client claims, hypotheses and unknowns are kept separate
- [ ] Ends with the stop condition
