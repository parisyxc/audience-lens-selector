Audience Lens Selector

Before you research an audience, decide how to look at it. Give it a marketing brief — get back the one segmentation axis that would actually change your decision, why it beats the more obvious cut, and a research handoff your listening or interviews can test.

Input · a brief
Brand / offer — category, product, price, route to market
Marketing decision — what this audience work must inform
Current assumptions (optional) — who you think the audience is, and why
→
Output · an audience research lens brief
Primary lens — plus the tempting cut it rejected
Contrast cards — every group a hypothesis, each with a falsifier
Research handoff — P0–P2 dimensions for listening / interviews

Deliberately not: social listening · fictional personas · segment sizes · a final target audience. It stops once the research angle is set — so nothing downstream is built on a character nobody verified.

What it produces — all 6 parts

Run on a made-up brand so you can see the full brief. Note what's missing on purpose: no persona names, no quotes, no percentages. Every group is a hypothesis.

Brand / offer
Relay — an online marketplace for inspected, pre-owned premium road bikes, ~$1.5k–6k, shipped with a 14-day return (fictional)
Marketing decision
Traffic is healthy but first purchases lag. Next quarter: lead acquisition with value or with verified-condition proof? And build a spec-comparison tool or a guided fit-and-confidence flow?
Current assumptions
"Our buyers are cyclists aged 30–50 who want a premium bike for less." — evidence: ad-platform interest targeting + founder intuition
Part 1Decision to informoutput: one-sentence frame
We need to understand differences in how much proof people need before buying a used premium bike online so the team can decide whether to lead with value or verified condition — and build a spec tool or a guided flow — without assuming that "cyclists aged 30–50" is the segment, or that price is the barrier.

Tempting cuts rejected
✕ Age 30–50 cyclists
Demographic default. The brief's own assumption. Age describes who shows up; it doesn't explain why one visitor checks out and another abandons. Kept as a descriptor.
✕ Bargain hunters vs premium seekers
No separation. Everyone on a resale marketplace is price-motivated — and the drop-off happens after they've already seen the lower price. Price is the shared baseline, not the divider.
Confidence: medium — the brief supports a proof barrier, but expertise and evidence threshold could swap places. The one question that decides it: among abandoners, do they drop before or after opening the inspection report?

Part 5Downstream research handoffoutput: what the next tool should go and test
Main question
What proof does each visitor need before paying for a used premium bike online — and does that need split by expertise?
Contrast corpora
Listing Q&A and support chats from buyers vs abandoners · forum threads on buying used carbon online vs first-upgrade threads
Look for
Component vocabulary · trust questions (crash history, scans, returns) · counter-signal: newcomers who never mention trust
Likely confounds
Price band (dearer bikes invite caution regardless of expertise) · frame material — carbon vs alloy may drive the proof need more than the person does · seasonality
Validate before naming a target
That these groups show up in Relay's own funnel data, roughly how often, and that abandonment really sits at the proof step — not the price step
Part 6Evidence ledger & clarificationoutput: facts, claims, hypotheses and unknowns kept separate
FHealthy traffic, weak first-purchase conversion · $1.5k–6k price band · 14-day returns · partner-mechanic inspection
C"Buyers are cyclists aged 30–50 who want a premium bike for less"
HExpertise and evidence threshold separate buying behaviour · some experts are carbon-wary · newcomers leave over fit and trust, not price
UWhere in the funnel people abandon · share of first-premium buyers · whether frame material outweighs the person
Questions that could change the lens
  1. Can you see drop-off before vs after the inspection report is opened?
  2. Roughly what share of live listings are carbon frames?
  3. Do support chats already get sizing and fit questions?
Stop condition: the research angle is specified; no audience insight, final persona, segment size or targeting decision has been claimed.
