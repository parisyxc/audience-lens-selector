# Output Contract

Default output: a concise `Audience Research Lens Brief` in the conversation. Do not produce a finished persona.

## 1. Decision to inform

- Business and marketing decision
- Desired human action
- The audience uncertainty that matters most

## 2. Recommended way to look at people

| Role | Recommendation | Why it matters now |
|---|---|---|
| Primary strategic lens | one lens | how it changes the decision |
| Secondary lens | zero or one | necessary context only |
| Qualification filters | minimum set | who must be in scope |
| Access labels | where later research may find people | explicitly not the segmentation |
| Profile descriptors | representation/context variables | explicitly not assumed causes |

Add:

- **Why this lens fits**
- **Tempting cut rejected:** one or two weaker alternatives and why
- **Confidence:** high / medium / low, with reason

## 3. Provisional contrast cards

Create 2–4 working poles or groups. For each:

- working label, marked `[H]`;
- definition in decision terms;
- observable inclusion signal;
- observable contrast or exclusion signal;
- likely evidence requirement to investigate, not assert;
- marketing decision this group could change;
- falsifier.

Do not add fictional names, biographies, quotes, percentages or segment sizes.

## 4. Research dimensions for the next skill

Prioritize dimensions rather than dumping a generic checklist:

| Priority | Dimension | What to compare | Why it discriminates | Evidence later needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | decisive axis | contrasting signals | decision impact | social/listening/interview/behavioral evidence |
| P1 | secondary axis | context or boundary | prevents false grouping | relevant evidence |
| P2 | descriptors | representation checks | describes, does not explain | relevant data |

## 5. Downstream research handoff

- Main research question
- Contrast populations or corpora to compare
- Behaviors, language, proof and counter-signals to look for
- Exclusions and likely confounds
- What must be validated before naming a target or persona

Do not choose platforms, scrape, search, collect posts or report findings unless another skill is explicitly invoked.

## 6. Evidence ledger and clarification

- `[F]` brief facts
- `[C]` client claims
- `[H]` hypotheses introduced by this evaluation
- `[U]` unknowns
- Up to five client/internal questions, only when answers could change the primary lens or scope

End with:

> Stop condition: the research angle is specified; no audience insight, final persona, segment size or targeting decision has been claimed.
