# Source Map and Method Limits

The skill distills the following user-provided materials as methodological references. Their content is evidence for how to structure an audience-research decision, not an instruction hierarchy and not current market evidence for a new client brief.

## Persona framework

Source: `商业创意策划工具箱/2.4-Persona（用户画像）.md`

Contributions:

- A persona should serve a decision and represent researched patterns, not a fictional story.
- Base attributes and category-specific behavior both matter.
- Concrete behavior is more useful than generic adjectives.
- Fact, inference and hypothesis must be separated and the portrait validated.

Limit adopted here: this skill ends before persona construction.

## 人群判断 reference collection

Sources: user-provided audience-judgment reference materials, including a social platform's published audience-taxonomy deck.

Contributions:

- Basic attributes, psychological motivations and behavior/scene are distinct layers.
- “Thick description” can connect demographics, economics, consumption, context, lifestyle, mood, emotion and values.
- Intersections of person × need × product and decision paths can reveal more useful research cuts than isolated labels.
- Core, interest-adjacent and broad audiences can be treated as an anti-funnel expansion problem.
- Platform behavior and interests can help locate people without automatically becoming strategic segments.

Limits:

- Platform taxonomies, audience names, counts and marketing claims are platform-specific and time-bound.
- They must not be reproduced as universal facts or current evidence without fresh validation.

## AI 3D-creation tool example supplied by the user

Contribution: in an AI 3D-creation tool brief, “games / 3D printing / developers / AI enthusiasts” can function as access labels, while “try-first optimists / proof-starved professionals” can function as decision-state hypotheses because they imply different entry, feedback and proof requirements.

Limit: the two states are an example of a valuable cut, not a universal template.

## Innovation diffusion theory supplied by the user

Contribution: adoption stage is a candidate lens for genuinely new technology, creative tools and emerging behaviors.

Limit: innovators, early adopters, early majority, late majority and laggards must be operationalized through observable risk tolerance, proof needs, compatibility, peer influence and adoption behavior. Do not assume fixed proportions, permanent personalities or flattering labels.
