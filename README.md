# Audience Lens Selector

> **EN** — Before you research an audience, decide how to look at it.
>
> **中文** —— 在研究人群**之前**，先判断该从哪个切口看人。

---

## 1 · 是什么 · What this is

**中文** —— **Audience Lens Selector**（人群视角判断）输入一份营销 brief，输出一份**人群研究视角 brief**：这次该用哪一条主轴去切人群（需求 / 痛点、决策心态、场合、品类专业度、采用阶段……），为什么是它、为什么不是那个更顺手的切法，以及交给下游 social listening / 访谈 / 问卷时具体去对比什么。它回答的不是「目标人群是谁」，而是**「研究人群之前，该怎么看人」**。

**EN** — Give it a marketing brief and it returns an **Audience Research Lens Brief**: the one segmentation axis that would actually change your decision, why it beats the more obvious cut, and a research handoff that listening, interviews or surveys can go and test. It doesn't answer "who is the target audience" — it answers **"how should we look at people before we research them."**

---

## 2 · Input → Output 契约 · The contract

### INPUT（3 项 · 3 fields — the second is the blocker）

| | 中文 | EN |
|---|---|---|
| **Brand / offer** | 品类、产品、价格带、渠道 / 通路 | Category, product, price band, route to market |
| **Marketing decision** | 这次人群研究要支撑**哪个决策**，希望人做出什么行为改变 | **Which decision** this audience work must inform, and what behaviour change you want |
| **Current audience assumptions** *(可选 / optional)* | 你现在以为的目标人群是谁，证据是什么 | Who you think the audience is, and on what evidence |

信息不全时带着显式假设继续，只追问会改变主轴选择的问题（≤ 5 个）。
Missing information → it proceeds with explicit assumptions and asks only questions that could change the lens (≤ 5).

### OUTPUT — Audience Research Lens Brief（6 部分 · 6 parts）

| # | 部分 · Part | 内容 · Contents |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **决策框定 · Decision to inform** | 「我们需要理解 ___ 上的差异，好让团队决定 ___，而不预设 ___。」 / *We need to understand differences in ___ so the team can decide ___ without assuming ___.* |
| 2 | **推荐的看人方式 · Way to look at people** | 主轴 · 副轴 · 资格过滤 · 触达标签 · 画像描述 + 打分 + **被否决的诱人切法** + 置信度 / primary & secondary lens, filters, access labels, descriptors, scoring, rejected cuts, confidence |
| 3 | **临时对照卡 · Contrast cards** | 2–4 组，全部标 `[H]`，每组都有**证伪条件** / 2–4 groups, all hypotheses, each with a falsifier |
| 4 | **研究维度 · Research dimensions** | P0 / P1 / P2 优先级 — prioritised, not a checklist |
| 5 | **下游交接 · Research handoff** | 主问题 · 对比语料 · 要找的信号与反信号 · 混淆因素 / main question, corpora, signals & counter-signals, confounds |
| 6 | **证据台账 · Evidence ledger** | `[F]` 事实 · `[C]` 客户说法 · `[H]` 假设 · `[U]` 未知 + stop condition |

> **一句话：给一份 brief，拿回一个「该怎么切人群」的研究角度 —— 以及它会怎样被证伪。**
> **In one line: put in a brief, get back a research angle — and exactly what result would prove it wrong.**

## 3 · 核心引擎 · What makes it reliable

**① 四类人群变量分离 · Four kinds of audience variable** —— 最锋利的一刀 / the sharpest idea:

| 类型 · Kind | 回答的问题 · Question | 例子 · Example |
|---|---|---|
| **策略主轴 · Strategic lens** | 什么差异解释了**不同的反应**？ / What difference explains a different response? | 先试再说 vs 要证据才动 / try-first vs proof-starved |
| 资格过滤 · Qualification filter | 谁必须在范围内？ / Who must be in scope? | 在首发市场、掌握预算 / in the launch market, controls budget |
| 触达标签 · Access label | 之后去**哪里找到**他们？ / Where can research find them? | 游戏、开发者、兴趣社区 / gaming, developer, interest communities |
| 画像描述 · Descriptor | 用什么来描述、检查代表性？ / What describes or checks representation? | 年龄、城市级别、职业 / age, city tier, profession |

「找得到」不等于「为什么行动」。*Findable is not the same as why they act.*

**② 12 条视角库 · 12-lens library** — 需求 / 痛点 · 决策心态 · 场合 · 品类专业度 · 采用阶段 · 转换阶段 · 人生转折 · 决策角色 · 身份 / 文化取向 · 反漏斗距离 · 经济性 · 忠诚度（`references/lens-library.md`）

**③ 六项 0–2 打分 · Six-criterion scoring** — 决策相关性 · 解释区分度 · 可观察性 · 可行动性 · 非冗余 · 证据可信度。分数用来**暴露判断**，不用来模拟确定性；决策相关性或可观察性得 0 的视角不能当主轴。
*The score exposes judgment rather than simulating certainty; a 0 on decision relevance or observability disqualifies a lens from primary.*

**④ 状态 vs 场景 · State vs scene** — 同一场景里的人需要不同的证据 → 选状态；场景本身改变了需求 → 选场景。
*Same scene, different reassurance needed → choose state; the setting itself changes the need → choose scene.*

**⑤ 最少推荐规则 · Minimum recommendation** — 1 个主轴、≤ 1 个副轴、2–4 个对照；判断不了时给出两条竞争视角 + 一个能决出胜负的问题。
*One primary lens, at most one secondary, 2–4 contrasts — or the two best rivals plus the single question that decides between them.*

## 4 · 明确不做 · Deliberately *not*

工具**在推荐研究角度之后就停下**。*It stops once the research angle is set.*

- 不爬数据、不做 social listening · *no scraping or social listening*
- 不声称洞察已被验证 · *no claim that an insight is validated*
- **不写虚构 persona** —— 没有名字、传记、引语 · ***no fictional personas*** *— no names, biographies or quotes*
- 不估算人群规模或占比，不选定最终目标人群 · *no segment sizes, no final target*
- 不因平台有这个标签就推荐按它投放 · *no media targeting just because a platform label exists*

九种失败模式 · Nine failure modes it avoids: 人口统计默认 demographic default · 兴趣分类学 interest taxonomy · **persona 表演 persona theatre** · 矩阵爆炸 matrix explosion · 固定类型谬误 fixed-type fallacy · 场景炫目 scene glamour · 创新者表演 innovation theatre · 过早定目标 premature targeting · 平台标签绝对化 platform absolutism.

> Stop condition: the research angle is specified; no audience insight, final persona, segment size or targeting decision has been claimed.

## 6 · 怎么用 · Use it

**中文** —— 把 `SKILL.md` 作为 skill 载入 → 给一份 brief（至少写清楚要支撑的决策）→ 拿回 6 部分 brief → 把第 5 部分交给 listening / 访谈 / 问卷去验证。建议先打开 `demo/` 看产出形态。

**EN** — Load `SKILL.md` as a skill, give it a brief (at minimum: the decision it must inform), get the 6-part brief back, then hand Part 5 to your listening, interview or survey workflow. Open `demo/` first to see the output shape.

A minimal brief:

```
Brand / offer: an online marketplace for inspected used premium road bikes, $1.5k–6k
Marketing decision: lead next quarter with value or with verified-condition proof?
Current assumption: "our buyers are cyclists aged 30–50 who want a premium bike for less"
```
